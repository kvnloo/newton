Use Newton's half-millimeter fallback particle radius for USD volume deformables (`UsdGeom.TetMesh` with `PhysicsVolumeDeformableSimAPI`) when `ModelBuilder.default_particle_radius` remains at its stock 0.1 stage-unit value. The fallback is `0.0005 / metersPerUnit` in stage units, matching the cloth and cable default-thickness convention. Deliberately changing the builder radius before `add_usd()` still overrides the volume fallback; legacy bare TetMeshes remain unchanged. To restore an exact 0.1 stage-unit radius for a simulation TetMesh, overwrite its `ModelBuilder.particle_radius` entries after import (use the `path_soft_map` result of `add_usd(..., return_deformable_results=True)` to locate the range).
