Invariant

Before any joint coordinate crosses joint_limit_lower/upper, Featherstone and
MuJoCo Euler agree to about 1e-5 on qd and 1e-7 on q (serial arm) or better
(cartpole). On every recorded step, Featherstone's joint_qdd matches H^{-1} tau
to a float32 residual (6e-4 absolute on O(1e2) accelerations; 6.8 absolute on
a 4.2e5 acceleration). H is symmetric to ~1e-9 and positive definite
(min eigenvalue > 0) on the serial arm. That rules out a broken CRBA product
or a bad Cholesky sign as the first failure.

The first violated condition is the explicit-penalty stability restriction
    dt <~ 2 / sqrt(limit_ke / lambda_min(H))
On the 6-DoF arm, lambda_min(H)=9.5954746e-06 and limit_ke=1e4, so the bound
is about 6.2e-5 s and the timestep used is 4.167e-3 s (~67x larger). The
spring is applied to the pre-step coordinate (jcalc_tau / joint_force). The
step that first exceeds the stop therefore still integrates the unbiased
acceleration; the next step sees the full ke*penetration - kd*qd and the
symplectic Euler update leaves the limit by O(10) radians in one step.
MuJoCo, with the same model, keeps the joint at the stop under both
integrator="euler" and integrator="implicitfast", so the split is limit
enforcement (explicit spring vs MuJoCo constraint impedance), not Featherstone
vs MuJoCo rigid-body bias forces, and not chain length by itself.

Repo quadruped and humanoid passive free-falls do not reach a finite joint
stop in 2 s, and they do not diverge. A serial arm with the same integrator
also does not diverge when gravity torque is zero or when the hinges are
effectively unlimited (cartpole tilt). The morphology correlation in the
upstream report is consistent with long arms sweeping into stiff limit_ke
springs on small generalized inertia; it is not evidence that the articulated-
body recurrence is special-cased wrong for serial chains.

angular_damping 0.05 vs 0.0 does not move the explosion step.
joint_armature=0.1 prevents the blow-up on this arm but the trajectory still
leaves MuJoCo by max|dq|=0.0106 at t=0.454 s, because the limit law is still
a different model.

No solver patch: a one-line clamp or an implicit limit would change the
published explicit-spring semantics, and the mass-matrix solve is already
consistent with tau. Upstream assets from SO-ARM100 were not in this repo and
were not rerun; the serial arm above is a Newton ModelBuilder model with the
same joint-limit parameters ModelBuilder uses by default (limit_ke=1e4,
limit_kd=10).
